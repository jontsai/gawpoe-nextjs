<p>Texas-based Outlaw Laboratory and its lawyers filed dozens of lawsuits and threatened hundreds of small shopkeepers selling rival sex supplements, netting more than $1.1 million in settlements</p>



<p>By Greg Land Staff Reporter  (September 18, 2020 at 04:05 PM)</p>



<p>A federal judge in California rejected the arguments of a law firm facing racketeering claims and potential class action for its part in a nationwide litigation campaign that netted more than $1 million from merchants threatened with lawsuits for selling “illegal” sexual enhancement products.</p>



<p>Beginning in 2017, Texas-based Outlaw Laboratory and its counsel,&nbsp;<a href="https://www.taulersmith.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Tauler Smith</a>&nbsp;in Los Angeles, sent thousands of letters to convenience store owners and “mom-and-pop” merchants selling competing products, warning that they were “selling illegal sexual enhancement drugs”&nbsp;and could be subject to “legal action for racketeering” and violations of the federal Lanham Act.</p>
