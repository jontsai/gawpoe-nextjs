<p>Approved for the development of a six-story building&nbsp;<a href="https://socketsite.com/archives/2013/05/376_castro_street_the_final_design.html">back in 2013</a>, with 24 residential units over ground floor retail and a 14-car garage to rise,&nbsp;<a href="https://socketsite.com/archives/2011/05/376_castro_plans_to_build_six_stories_24_units_back_in.html">the prominent gas station site on the northwest corner of Castro and Market</a>&nbsp;has been locked in&nbsp;<a href="https://socketsite.com/archives/2015/04/two-year-battle-over-prominent-castro-site-could-soon-be-concluded.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">an epic legal battle</a>&nbsp;ever since.</p>



<p>And after two years of litigation and a deadlocked jury, Superior Court judge Suzanne Bolanos has just ruled in favor of the majority owner-operators of the site and against developer Joe Tierney who paid $340,000 in exchange for a 10 percent interest in the property back in 2004 with an agreement to purchase the remainder of the site for $3,094,000.</p>
