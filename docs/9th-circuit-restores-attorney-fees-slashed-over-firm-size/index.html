February 26, 2026

The 9th U.S. Circuit Court of Appeals has vacated a $4.5 million fee reduction, ruling that a winning law firm’s size cannot be used to justify lower fees and declaring that “brilliance at the bar” is not measure by the number of associates a lawyer commands.
