<h4 class="wp-block-heading h-in-pg" id="summary">Summary</h4>



<p>Christopher Wimmer joined Gaw | Poe LLP in August 2025 and has been practicing law since graduating in 2005 from Columbia Law School. He has first-chaired nine trials and arbitrations, argued appeals in the California Courts of Appeal and the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit, and filed briefs with the Supreme Court of California (as lead merits counsel) and the Supreme Court of the United States (as lead amicus counsel). He has also been lead transactional counsel on commercial, financing, and M&amp;A contracts in the cannabis industry cumulatively worth over $850 million.</p>



<div class="wp-block-buttons is-layout-flex wp-block-buttons-is-layout-flex">
<div class="wp-block-button"><a class="wp-block-button__link wp-element-button" href="/fractional-general-counsel/">View Fractional General Counsel Services</a></div>
</div>



<p><br>Prior to joining Gaw | Poe LLP, Mr. Wimmer spent thirteen years at elite commercial litigation firms in New York and San Francisco and seven years as in-house and external litigation, transactional, and regulatory counsel in the cannabis industry. </p>



<p>While at Columbia Law School, Mr. Wimmer received the Paul R. Hays Prize for civil procedure along with numerous other honors and served as a teaching assistant for the late Jack Greenberg. He also clerked for the late Hon. Jack B. Weinstein. Before attending Columbia, Mr. Wimmer graduated from U.C. Berkeley with a dual degree in Rhetoric (with Honors) and Molecular &amp; Cell Biology. He has been published academically and professionally on a range of topics including litigation publicity and California’s Anti-SLAPP statute, the history of criminal sentencing in the U.S. from the colonial era to present, the Supreme Court’s Seventh Amendment jurisprudence, legal and ethical issues in correctional psychiatry and guardianships, and the Russian novelist Vladimir Nabokov.</p>
