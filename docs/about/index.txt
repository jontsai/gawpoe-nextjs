<h2 class="wp-block-heading h-standard dlrg">Our Story</h2>



<p>We win trials. We win appeals. And we do both more efficiently than the large law firms you could hire to get worse results. That is why we are one of a handful of small firms in California to be ranked in the Chambers &amp; Partners USA Guide, 2026—the most recent edition of what the National Law Review has described as “<a href="https://natlawreview.com/article/it-s-chambers-season-what-you-want-to-know-megan-braverman-and-host-sharon-berman" data-type="link" data-id="https://natlawreview.com/article/it-s-chambers-season-what-you-want-to-know-megan-braverman-and-host-sharon-berman"><em>one of the legal industry’s most prestigious rankings — and also the most notoriously difficult to crack.</em></a>”</p>



<p><br>Gaw | Poe LLP was founded by Stanford Law School graduates Randolph Gaw and Mark Poe to handle complex antitrust, business, and catastrophic injury litigation.&nbsp; The firm has prevailed in numerous trials, arbitrations, and appeals—achieving more than $93 million in value for their clients, including the 37th largest jury verdict in the United States during the year 2016.&nbsp; In addition, the firm has successfully obtained more than $97 million in value for their clients through settlements in other cases. Due to their successful advocacy, both Randolph and Mark<br>were honored by Chambers &amp; Partners with individual rankings—a rarity for small firm lawyers practicing in large legal markets.</p>



<h3 class="wp-block-heading h-in-pg">At Gaw | Poe LLP, we take pride in following five core principles that form our approach to litigation:</h3>



<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>We are ethical.</strong>&nbsp;We believe in zealous advocacy, but we will not compromise our standards of professional responsibility or common decency.&nbsp; This makes us more effective advocates and gives credibility to our clients’ claims and defenses.</li>



<li><strong>We are candid.</strong>&nbsp;We will give frank assessments of the merits of your case and its progress.&nbsp; We will provide our best estimates for budgets instead of providing an artificially low guess designed to win business.&nbsp; We expect our clients to communicate honestly with us and we promise the same in return.</li>



<li><strong>We are cost-effective.</strong>&nbsp;Our attorneys are graduates of elite law schools and/or have years of prior litigation experience with top-ranked law firms.&nbsp; At the same time, our billing rates are comparable to the billing rates charged by large law firms—whom we regularly face—for their inexperienced junior lawyers. &nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>We take risks.</strong> We are not afraid of trials.&nbsp; We employ unconventional strategies that other lawyers don’t even think about.&nbsp; And we will offer to share the risk with our clients.&nbsp; For clients with multimillion-dollar claims, we offer both full and partial contingency fee options. &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;</li>



<li><strong>We get results.</strong>&nbsp;The four principles we’ve identified above would not matter if we didn’t achieve good outcomes.&nbsp;We do. </li>
</ol>



<p>Prior to starting the firm, Randolph and Mark enjoyed a combined 20 years of experience in handling complex commercial litigation with international law firms O’Melveny &amp; Myers, Morrison &amp; Foerster, and Wilson Sonsini Goodrich &amp; Rosati.&nbsp; They opened Gaw | Poe LLP with the goal of providing an efficient, hands-on approach to litigation without sacrificing the quality expected of an Am Law 50 firm.</p>
