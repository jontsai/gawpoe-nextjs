<p>Texas-based Outlaw Laboratory has teamed up with Los Angeles&#8217; Tauler Smith to sue hundreds of convenience stores and gas stations claiming they sell adulturated sex supplements. Defense lawyers are striking back, calling the claims a &#8220;shakedown.&#8221;</p>



<p>By Greg Land | July 15, 2019 at 09:00 AM</p>



<p>The original version of this story was published on&nbsp;<a href="https://www.law.com/dailyreportonline/">Daily Report Online</a></p>



<p>A Texas-based manufacturer of men’s sexual enhancement products is on the defensive after many of the targets of its national litigation barrage against retailers of rival products&nbsp;decided to punch back, calling its settlement offers a “shakedown.”</p>



<p>Outlaw Laboratory seemed&nbsp;to have discovered a winning formula when it teamed up with a California law firm to file dozens of multidefendant lawsuits against convenience stores, gas stations and retailers around the country, asserting claims for false advertising over competing sex-drive products on the stores’ shelves.&nbsp;</p>
