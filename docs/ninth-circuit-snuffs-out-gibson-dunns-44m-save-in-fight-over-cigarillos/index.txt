<p>It’s a reminder for appellate lawyers everywhere: No matter how aggressive or creative you are, you’re still stuck with the lower court record.</p>



<p>By Jenna Greene | February 12, 2019 at 09:00 AM</p>



<p>The original version of this story was published on&nbsp;<a href="https://www.law.com/litigationdaily/litigationdaily/">Litigationdaily/litigationdaily</a></p>



<p>When Swisher International hired Gibson, Dunn &amp; Crutcher in 2016, the Florida-based tobacco company needed the legal equivalent of a white knight.</p>



<p>It had just been hit with a $44 million jury verdict in an antitrust suit, plus a couple million more in legal fees for plaintiffs counsel from upstart San Francisco litigation boutique&nbsp;<a href="https://www.law.com/litigationdaily/2018/06/06/daily-dicta-dream-of-leaving-big-law-to-launch-a-litigation-boutique-these-two-lawyers-made-it-work/">Gaw Poe</a>, founded by alums from Morrison &amp; Foerster and O’Melveny &amp; Myers.</p>
