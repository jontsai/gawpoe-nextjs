<p>Gaw | Poe LLP Successfully Defends Jury Verdict On Appeal</p>



<p>SAN FRANCISCO –&nbsp;January 3, 2019&nbsp;– Gaw | Poe LLP announced&nbsp;today&nbsp;that it successfully defended its jury verdict in favor of client Donald Okada after that verdict was appealed to the Ninth Circuit Court of Appeals by defendant Mark Whitehead.&nbsp; In December 2016, a jury in Orange County federal court found on all counts in favor of Mr. Okada in his lawsuit alleging breach of contract and fraud by Mr. Whitehead in connection with a real estate transaction.&nbsp; The final judgment awarded Mr. Okada over $1 million in damages and attorney’s fees, and denied all relief to Mr. Whitehead on his $7 million worth of counterclaims.</p>



<p>Mr. Whitehead appealed the jury verdict to the Ninth Circuit and requested that it be vacated on various grounds.&nbsp; In a short 5-page memorandum opinion, the Ninth Circuit panel unanimously agreed with Mr. Okada’s arguments and upheld the jury verdict and final judgment.&nbsp;</p>



<p>The case is Donald Okada v. Mark Whitehead, No. 17-56007, and was heard in the United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit.&nbsp; Donald Okada was represented by Gaw | Poe LLP attorneys Randolph Gaw and Victor Meng.</p>
