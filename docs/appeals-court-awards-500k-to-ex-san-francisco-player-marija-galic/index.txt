<p>September 4, 2025</p>



<p>A California appeals court has reinstated $500,000 in punitive damages for former University of San Francisco women’s basketball player Marija Galic and ordered a new trial for her twin sister, Marta, in a case alleging years of verbal and emotional abuse by head coach Molly Goodenbour.</p>
