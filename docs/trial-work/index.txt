<h2 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-center h-standard dlrg">Trial Work</h2>



<p>Trial lawyers are separated from litigators by virtue of their ability to connect with a jury, their judgment and sixth sense when it comes to courtroom optics and trial strategy, and their ability to think quickly on their feet.&nbsp; All trial lawyers are litigators, but few litigators are trial lawyers. &nbsp;</p>



<p>Our results in trials and arbitrations speak for themselves and are conclusive proof that we are not just litigators, but true trial lawyers.&nbsp; We are happy to be brought in as trial counsel on short notice when a client needs some extra firepower to get the results they deserve, and also on a contingency fee basis.&nbsp;</p>
