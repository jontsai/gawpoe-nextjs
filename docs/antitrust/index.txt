  <h2 class="wp-block-heading has-text-align-center h-standard dlrg" style="margin:0;">Antitrust</h2>
</div>



<p>Fair business competition is a crucial American value.&nbsp; The history of American enterprise is that of new businesses succeeding in the marketplace through innovation and lowering costs to consumers.&nbsp; The federal and state antitrust laws exist to promote such healthy business competition, and we represent clients bringing antitrust claims like:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Sherman Act</strong> claims for monopolization and attempted monopolization.&nbsp; This is when a business with dominant market power unlawfully uses underhanded business tactics against its less powerful competitors.</li>



<li><strong>Robinson-Patman Act</strong> claims for federal price discrimination.&nbsp; Suppliers are lawfully supposed to offer the same price to all wholesalers or retailers operating at the same level in the distribution chain, but some suppliers illegally favor certain buyers over others by giving them exclusive discounts, promotions, or other benefits.</li>
</ul>
