<h4 class="wp-block-heading h-in-pg" id="summary">Summary</h4>



<p>Flora Vigo is Of Counsel with Gaw | Poe LLP and has been with the firm since 2018. She is a litigator with a diverse background ranging from prosecution and defense of class actions to trade secret and complex business disputes. Ms. Vigo has been trial counsel of record on several jury trials.</p>



<p>Prior to joining Gaw | Poe LLP, Ms. Vigo was Counsel at the international law firm of O’Melveny &amp; Myers LLP, where she successfully represented clients in class actions, breach of fiduciary duty suits, and commercial litigation.</p>



<p>Ms. Vigo has been recognized as a Rising Star™ for Northern California for 2013 and 2014, by Super Lawyers magazine. Ms. Vigo graduated with distinction from UC Hastings College of the Law, where she was elected to the Thurston Society and earned the Witkin Award for Excellence. Ms. Vigo received her undergraduate degree from the University of California, Santa Cruz.</p>
