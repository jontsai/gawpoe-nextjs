Business Litigation

We handle all types of high-stakes commercial litigation, including cases where tens or hundreds of millions of dollars are at stake.  We represent both plaintiffs and defendants, as well as both companies and individuals.  Virtually any sophisticated business dispute is within our scope of practice, such as:

  • Enforcement of contract terms, including earnout clauses, option agreements, exclusive rights clauses, non-competes, and indemnification agreements. 
  • Fraud, racketeering, breach of fiduciary duty, and tortious interference with contract claims. 
  • Trade libel, product disparagement, unfair competition, trade secrets misappropriation and intellectual property infringement claims. 
    Class actions brought by either consumers or businesses.
