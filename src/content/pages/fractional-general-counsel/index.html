FRACTIONAL GENERAL COUNSEL

We offer fractional General Counsel services to companies of any size. We have worked with private and publicly traded companies in a wide array of industries, from technology and finance to CPG, and have been lead transactional counsel on commercial, financing, and M&A contracts cumulatively worth over $850 million.

We provide practical, efficient advice about the legal issues confronting sophisticated businesses.

Areas of Focus

  • Governance, including Board of Director and stockholder votes and management decision-making.
  • Commercial deals, including wholesale purchase, management services, SaaS, equipment financing, consulting, processing, and other agreements.
  • Loan agreements, including secured and convertible debt.
  • M&A, including stock purchases and asset purchase agreements.
  • Real estate, including purchase, lease, and sublease agreements.
  • Human resources, including employee, contractor, and union relations.
  • IP, including licensing and confidentiality agreements.

  • Securities, including public reporting.
